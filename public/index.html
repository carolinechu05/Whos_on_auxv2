🎵 HOW TO PLAY WHO'S ON AUX

👤 1. JOIN THE GAME
Enter your name to join the wheel. Your name will appear on the spinning wheel with all other players.
🗳️ 2. VOTE FOR AUX CONTROL
When voting starts, click on other players to vote for who should control the music. The person with the most votes wins!
🎧 3. THE WINNER CONTROLS THE MUSIC
The AUX holder can click song tiles on the wheel to play them, pause/resume playback, and adjust volume for everyone.
😊 4. REACT IN REAL-TIME
Use the live reaction buttons at the bottom to show how you feel about the song as it plays. Everyone can see reactions in real-time!
⭐ 5. RATE THE MUSIC
After the song ends, rate it with emoji reactions (😖 to 🤩). Ratings determine if the AUX holder keeps control or if someone new takes over.
⚡ PRO TIPS
• Vote wisely - they control the vibe!
• React live to show your feelings
• Watch the countdown for timing
• AUX holder rotates based on ratings
• Have fun and discover new music!
